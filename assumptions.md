# Assumptions and compliance notes

## Purpose

This calculator is a modelling aid for Divergent Wealth. It is designed to show how changes in interest rates, loan terms, property values, rent and ownership costs may affect a home loan or investment-property scenario.

It is not intended to replace advice, lending assessment, tax advice or a product comparison.

## Model assumptions

### Loan repayments

- The calculator models principal and interest repayments only.
- Repayments are estimated using the standard amortisation formula.
- Interest is assumed to compound at the same frequency as the selected repayment frequency.
- Interest rates are assumed to remain constant within each scenario.
- The stress-tested rate is calculated by adding the entered stress percentage to the entered current interest rate.
- The lower-rate scenario is calculated by subtracting the stress percentage, with a floor of 0%.

### Offset and extra repayments

- The offset balance reduces the modelled interest base.
- The offset balance does not reduce the scheduled contractual repayment.
- Extra repayments are applied per selected repayment period.
- The projected balance calculation is simplified and intended for scenario comparison only.

### Property projection

- Property growth is compounded annually using the rate entered by the user.
- Projected equity is calculated as projected property value less projected loan balance.
- The calculator does not forecast property values. The growth rate is an assumption entered by the user.

### Owner-occupier mode

- Annual ownership costs are treated as cash outflows.
- The calculator does not include stamp duty, conveyancing, lender fees, refinancing costs, insurance differences, maintenance shocks, council-rate changes or opportunity cost.

### Investment mode

- Rent is annualised from weekly rent and reduced by the vacancy allowance entered.
- Property management fee is calculated as a percentage of annual rent.
- Before-tax cashflow is rent less loan repayments, annual ownership costs and property management fee.
- Indicative after-tax cashflow is simplified.
- The model assumes deductible expenses are deductible only to the percentage entered by the user.
- It does not model depreciation, capital works deductions, borrowing-cost amortisation, capital gains tax, land tax thresholds, negative-gearing law changes, trust/company ownership, GST, apportionment between co-owners, or private-use adjustments beyond the deductible percentage input.

## Compliance boundaries

This calculator must be described as general information only.

It must not be used to say:

- a client can afford a loan
- a client should borrow a specific amount
- a client should buy, hold, sell or refinance a property
- a particular loan product is suitable
- a tax deduction is definitely available
- a specific strategy is appropriate for a client without advice work

Preferred wording:

> This calculator is a scenario-modelling tool. It does not take account of your personal objectives, financial situation, needs, credit position, tax position or legal circumstances.
